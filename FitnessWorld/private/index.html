A straightforward plan for a growing gym community.

  • Create/manage gym group

  • 2 credits in days 1–15
  • 2 credits in days 16–end of month
  • Basic member activity and leaderboard

ADVANCED GYM TOOLS

Gym Pro

₹499 / month

Advanced tools and a lower platform fee for sellers.

  • Everything in Beginner
  • 10 free challenge credits per billing month
  • 5 credits in days 1–15
  • 5 credits in days 16–end of month
  • Manage multiple groups (limits to be configured)
  • Advanced analytics and member progress reports
  • Custom gym branding and banners
  • Priority support
  • Marketplace selling platform fee: 1%
